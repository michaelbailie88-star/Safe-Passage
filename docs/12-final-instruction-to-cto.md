# Final Instruction to CTO.new

Build Safe Passage as a mission-driven startup, not simply a web application.

Every design decision should reinforce trust, clarity, privacy, and hope.

The experience should feel like entering a lighthouse during a storm: calm, welcoming, secure, and purposeful.

Prioritize scalability, maintainability, accessibility, and security from day one.

The product should be modular so future capabilities — mobile apps, AI coaching, community expansion, corporate wellness, and optional faith pathways — can be added without requiring a complete rewrite.

## North Star Principle
No man should have to navigate life's storms alone.
