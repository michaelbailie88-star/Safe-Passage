# Page 4 – The Solution

Safe Passage combines five core experiences:

## The Lighthouse
Personal dashboard.
- Daily emotional check-ins.
- Habit tracking.
- Goals.
- Progress.

## The Logbook
Private journaling.
- Guided reflections.
- Mood tracking.
- Gratitude.
- Fatherhood reflections.

## The Crossing
Guided transformation programs. Examples:
- Rebuild
- Fatherhood
- Purpose
- Relationships
- Confidence
- Faith (optional)

## The Watch
Community.
- Accountability.
- Discussion groups.
- Mentorship.
- Events.

## The Beacon
Support center.
- Trusted contacts.
- Professional resources.
- Safety information.
